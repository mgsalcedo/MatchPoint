# ADR-0010 — No social feed in PMV

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Sports communities already use Instagram, TikTok, Strava, and WhatsApp for social content. Building a feed would increase scope and compete with existing habits.

## Decision

MatchPoint PMV will not include a social feed.

## Rationale

Keeps the PMV focused on discovery and contact; avoids content moderation complexity; reduces development scope; prevents the product from becoming a generic social network.

## Consequences

No likes, comments, followers, or user posts (BR-005); organization photos can exist as profile content only; social features can be revisited after PMV validation (`docs/roadmap.md`'s backlog parking lot).
