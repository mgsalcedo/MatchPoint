# ADR-0009 — Match™ as guide, not chatbot

## Status

Accepted

## Context

MatchPoint needs to feel guided and personal, but a full chatbot would add complexity and risk.

## Decision

Match™ will be a lightweight digital guide in PMV, not a full conversational chatbot (see `docs/match-character.md`).

## Rationale

Adds personality without complexity; helps users feel supported; avoids hallucination risks; keeps the PMV focused.

## Consequences

Match™ appears through copy, helper text, loading states, and explanations (`docs/microcopy.md`); conversational AI is deferred to V3 (`docs/roadmap.md`'s "AI sports advisor" milestone).
